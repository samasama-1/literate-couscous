# Manual delivery balance requests and payment at collection

Prepared locally on 27 October 2026. No remote SQL, deployment, customer email, production setting change, staging or commit has been performed. See [28 October safety revisions](payment-rollout-hardening-2026-10-28.md) for the current flag compatibility, legacy-action rejection and required fee-off migration. This plan supersedes earlier instructions to send advance balance requests to self-collection customers. The wider launch/security checklist still applies.

## What admins do

### Collections Dashboard — /admin?mode=collection

1. Search by order code/customer details. Filters include preparing, ready, outstanding balance and collected. The list now includes verified-deposit orders still being prepared; it no longer hides unpaid collection orders.
2. Confirm the agreed **whole-order final total** for all units. The desk calculates the product balance from the verified deposit. No guessed tier price is prefilled. Saving a total does not email the customer, prove payment or release goods.
3. Mark the goods ready for collection when they actually are. Inventory readiness is separate from payment clearance. The existing ready notification can tell the owner the outstanding balance is payable at collection and include the named-collector form when enabled.
4. When the customer arrives, check the **actual credited funds in the business bank account**, match the exact remaining amount and transaction reference, and tick the verification attestation. A customer screenshot, promise or pending transfer is insufficient. Record the verification; admin identity, time and amount/reference are audited. The screen shows the last balance verifier.
5. Complete the separate existing identity/authorization, storage-clearance and single-handover check **before releasing goods**. The collection dashboard links to the full record for this check. Readiness alone never authorizes release; unchanged payment/storage guards remain enforced by the database.

The implemented desk method is bank/PayNow reconciliation. Cash, split/partial balances, combining product and storage charges in one receipt, and overpayment/refund handling are not silently introduced. Reconcile those exceptions through an approved operational procedure before release. Already-paid historical collection orders must never be charged again. A zero product balance needs no new payment. Existing storage charges, if applicable under approved accepted terms, remain separately reconciled.

### Delivery balance emails — /admin/delivery-payments

1. Open each delivery order and confirm its final total, including any **agreed** delivery charge. Selecting delivery alone does not imply the customer agreed to an amount.
2. Review the delivery list, select up to **25 orders**, and preview the recipient/product/quantity/total/deposit/balance summary.
3. Explicitly confirm the reviewed recipients, totals and charges, then queue that batch. Only this admin action creates new delivery balance email jobs. There is no new automatic balance-request producer, reminder or schedule.
4. The existing authenticated email worker delivers approved jobs with existing pacing, retries and uncertain-send review. Queued does not mean sent or delivered. A manual batch is not an immediate, unlimited bulk mail service; existing provider capacity still governs throughput. Check the individual order's email/CRM delivery history and Operations health.
5. Match delivery payments through the existing verified-payment action. Delivery must still be fully paid before being marked ready/delivered.

Each actual email retains the product image when valid/available, product/batch, quantity, order code, final total, verified deposit, balance, an encrypted-at-rest private payment link and hello@samasama.sg. The preview is an approval summary, not a guarantee of pixel-identical rendering. Final wording and owned-inbox acceptance are still launch review items. Missing product data fails safely in the worker; it must be corrected before retrying.

A current payment link blocks another request. Inspect/reconcile a failed or review job rather than creating duplicates. Changing the agreed total invalidates the old link and requires a fresh preview. Old pre-change queued requests fail current hash/state checks. Processing/uncertain requests block repricing and replacement until reviewed.

## Security and operating behavior

- Admin authentication precedes all business reads/mutations. Recipients and money never come from trusted hidden fields; SQL derives them from locked order records. Posted fields are limited to order IDs, revisions, consent and a request ID. Private tokens are generated/sealed server-side and are not returned in dashboard responses or sent to CRM.
- `balance_revision` invalidates stale previews after recipient/method/financial/order changes. The database rechecks verified deposit, exact totals, outstanding balance and delivery eligibility under locks. A mixed/changed batch rolls back entirely; it does not partly send.
- Batch IDs record actor + canonical ID/revision manifest. Retrying the same batch cannot overwrite links or add jobs. A reused ID with another selection fails. Overlapping batches lock orders in sorted order and use short lock timeouts; no network call occurs while locks are held.
- Payment verification uses the existing exact-amount/cross-payment-reference guards, with a required bank-credit attestation. Two clicks on the same order cannot create two balance receipts. This is staff bank reconciliation, **not an automatic bank confirmation API**.
- RPCs are owned by postgres, use `pg_catalog, pg_temp`, and deny public/anon/authenticated execution. Service-role access is restricted to the intended RPCs; manifest tables are not directly accessible through the API. No browser service key, token logging or credentials are added.
- Page size is 50 plus one look-ahead row; associated batches/products are limited to that page. Search requires at least three consecutive letters/digits and uses trigram indexes. There are no database-wide counts or OFFSET scans. Each request transaction touches at most 25 orders, with indexed lookups for pending/review jobs and recent payment audit.
- A delivery request per recipient adds one order update, one explicit email job, the existing CRM update, one balance-request audit and one manifest item; a batch adds one manifest row. Queue classification/counters are preserved if the runtime migration is installed. Collection total/readiness/payment are separate bounded transactions and retain the existing audit/CRM work; no advance collection balance-request/receipt email is produced. Readiness and existing order/deposit/security notifications remain separate.
- Completed email-job cleanup may delete a job while preserving its manifest item with a null job pointer; it is not blocked by this feature. Do not delete idempotency/audit records ad hoc. Their long-term financial/audit retention needs the existing retention review.

4. Reconcile existing positive-balance collection orders, historical deposit records, valid private links, ready notices and uncertain jobs. Do not backfill payments/readiness, invalidate customer links or replay old emails without explicit review. ## Staging acceptance plan

Use synthetic records and separate PostgreSQL sessions. Local PGlite executes concurrent promises serially and does not prove production transaction contention.

- Collection: verified deposit → explicitly confirmed total → ready while unpaid → handover blocked → exact bank amount/reference + attestation → ID/authorization/storage check → one recorded release. Verify owner and named collector paths, zero balance, already paid, missing historical deposit, wrong amount, reused bank reference, expired/revoked authorization and failed storage check.
- Race two payment submissions with the same order/revision/reference; one saves and the other stops. Race two orders using the same bank reference, payment versus refund/reprice, and two handovers. Confirm ledger/audit consistency and bounded lock waits.
- Delivery: select 1/25 eligible orders; preview and queue; reject collection rows, 26+, duplicates, unknown IDs, changed recipient/method/quantity/total, unverified deposit, paid/fulfilled/ready orders, and missing fields. Race overlapping/reordered batches; verify whole-batch rollback, same-ID retries, changed-manifest rejection and no token overwrite.
- Simulate an RPC response lost after commit and retry the exact request/manifest; no duplicate job. Simulate worker preparation failure/provider timeout; uncertain sends stay in review and do not get a new request. Confirm actual final email/image/private link against an approved owned inbox only after send approval.
- Test a balance paid after a ready job was queued: the readiness message must not demand an already-paid balance. Collection balance requests/receipts from older queued jobs must skip; delivery balance receipt/readiness and order/deposit messages remain correct. Check CRM `Balance due at collection`/`Ready — balance due at collection` mapping against configured single-line fields.
- Check anon/authenticated RPC denial, service permissions, function owner/search path, browser bundle/log redaction, restricted admin access, old private links, and indexed query plans on representative staging data. Delete a completed synthetic job through the reviewed cleanup and verify the manifest does not prevent it.
- Confirm queue priority/backlog/oldest job and existing provider pacing; do not increase provider limits to make a test batch appear instantaneous. During an outage, hold goods until the authoritative payment + current authorization + single-handover record can be confirmed; an emergency spreadsheet is not proof of a fresh bank credit or a synchronized handover lock.

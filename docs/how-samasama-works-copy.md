# How SamaSama Works — website copy draft

12 November 2026; contact/refund decisions updated 16 November. Draft for Marcus's review; no website changes or publishing. Companion: [customer email journey](customer-email-journey.md).

## Customer-facing page copy

### Good finds. Better prices, together.

SamaSama brings people together to buy carefully selected products in a shared order, called a batch. Combining demand helps us access volume pricing, while we handle supplier coordination, shipping and local fulfilment.

You join before the goods are ready for collection. That means there is a wait—and a few steps along the way. Here's what happens, what you're paying for, and what to expect from us.

**Browse open batches** · **See the SamaSama Standard**

### 1. We do the sourcing. You see what made the cut.

Our process starts before a batch opens: reviewing suppliers, checking product claims and samples, and assessing suitability for Singapore homes through the SamaSama Standard.

On each batch page, look for the product details, relevant checks, pricing tiers, deposit, estimated fulfilment window and support terms. We explain what has been checked and flag information that is still being confirmed, so you can decide whether the product is right for you.

**See how we select products**

### 2. Interested, but not ready to order?

Register your interest in a product and we'll record your pick. Registering interest doesn't place an order or require payment.

When our early-interest offer is available, your confirmation email also includes a personal code for **S$10 off each qualifying unit on your first paid order**. It applies to products with a final price of at least S$50 per unit before this discount, for up to ten units. Your code is valid for six calendar months from issue; use the same email address when ordering. The exact expiry is in your email.

The discount is taken off your product total. Your deposit stays the same. Choosing to receive future marketing updates is separate and optional.

### 3. Choose your batch and place a deposit.

When a batch opens, review its details and submit your order. You'll receive the deposit amount and payment instructions.

Your first email tells you we've received your order request. Submitting it does not reserve units. Your slot is secured only after we've verified that your deposit has reached our bank account and confirmed availability. Only verified orders count towards batch progress. If the capacity is unavailable after you pay, our team will reach out to let you know, and your deposit will be refunded via your original payment method.

Your deposit forms part of your payment for the order—it isn't an extra charge. It confirms genuine participation before we commit to the batch with the supplier.

### 4. More people join. Better prices can unlock.

Each batch shows its pricing tiers and the participation needed to reach them. As more verified orders join, the batch can unlock a lower final price under those published rules.

You don't need to wait for a lower tier to join. Your final product price follows the tier the batch achieves when pricing is locked, with any eligible personal discount shown separately.

Know someone who might like the product too? Share the batch page. Their participation could help everyone reach the next price tier.

Once the best tier is unlocked, that's the best price for the batch. Friends can still join while it remains open.

**Share this batch**

### 5. The batch closes. We confirm the outcome and price.

If the batch meets its requirements and proceeds, we'll confirm your final price, any applied discount, the deposit already paid and the remaining balance.

If the batch doesn't reach its minimum required participation, your deposit will be refunded in full within 10 business days from the date we confirm that the batch will not proceed. We'll explain the refund next steps and confirm when it has been completed.

Please read the batch's cancellation and refund terms before paying. Whether you can cancel after joining depends on the stage of the batch and the commitments already made to the supplier.

### 6. We coordinate the order and keep you informed.

Once the batch proceeds, we coordinate procurement or production, where needed, and shipping with the supplier and logistics partners.

Your batch's estimated fulfilment window is the guide. It is an estimate, rather than a guaranteed collection date: supplier schedules, freight, customs clearance and local checks can affect the timing.

We'll keep you informed as the order moves through its key stages:

- **Cargo shipped:** the shipment has departed and is on its way.
- **Local processing and inspection:** the goods are being processed and checked before release.
- **Ready:** your order has been released for collection or delivery arrangements.

Arrival in Singapore doesn't automatically mean an item is ready to collect. Please wait for your ready notification before making the trip.

If the expected timeline changes, we'll explain what we know, what happens next and when you can expect another update. You can contact us at **hello@samasama.sg** with your order code if you have questions.

### 7. Collect your order, or arrange delivery where available.

**Collecting yourself?** Your ready email will explain the location, collection arrangements and any remaining balance payable at collection. Payment must be verified before we hand over the goods. If you've already settled your balance, you won't be asked to pay it again.

If someone is collecting for you, follow the authorisation instructions provided. We'll explain what they need to bring. Your collection notice will also state any applicable collection deadlines and terms.

**Arranging delivery?** Where delivery is available, we'll confirm the arrangements, applicable charges and remaining payment steps with you. A ready notification means we can arrange the next step; it doesn't mean your order has already been dispatched.

### 8. Bring it home. We're here if you need help.

Keep your order confirmation and check the product's setup and care instructions before use.

If something isn't right, contact **hello@samasama.sg** with your order code and a description of the issue. We'll help you with the next steps under the warranty or replacement terms stated for your product.

Once you've had time to try it, we'd love your honest feedback. It helps us improve what we source—and helps the next person decide.

### Know the steps before you join.

Check the product, understand the batch, and ask us anything you're unsure about. You should know what you're committing to before you place a deposit.
